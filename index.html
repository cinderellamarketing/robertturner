2 One sales pipeline for both services▶

Enquiries for architecture and for party wall work follow the same steps. The enquiry comes in, you arrange a call, and you send the quote. So both services share one sales pipeline, and each enquiry is an opportunity in Capsule.

  • A quote stays in the pipeline until the client accepts it, because that is when they agree you are doing the work.
  • The pipeline answers questions such as how many enquiries came in, or how many quotes went out, in a given month.
3 Projects and stages▶

Once a quote is accepted, the job becomes a project. From there the two services run differently.

ServiceStages
ArchitecturePlanning, Building Regulations, Health and Safety
Party wallService of notice, Service of award

Stages can overlap. A job in a hurry may run planning and building regulations together, and planning conditions sometimes need dealing with during building regulations. The steps within each stage are a workflow of tasks, and several can run at the same time. Not every job follows every step in the same order.

At a glance

The overview you both look at shows five things for each project:

  • Project reference number
  • Address
  • Stage
  • Next task
  • Who the next task is with: one of you, the client, the planners or another specialist

Everything else stays on the project record, there whenever you open it.

4 Party wall notices▶

A party wall job can involve one adjoining owner or many, and each of them needs tracking separately. For each adjoining owner, the project records:

  • Whether the notice has been served
  • Their response: consent, which ends it for that owner, or dissent, which needs a party wall award
  • No response after 14 days: a further notice giving 10 days
  • No response after that: a surveyor appointed on their behalf

Each step is recorded on the project, with a reminder when each period runs out. That way, a notice not yet served on one owner shows up, rather than hiding behind one that has been.

Other people on the job, such as structural engineers, go on the project with their details. Your role on each side of a job, whether building owner's surveyor, adjoining owner's surveyor or agreed surveyor, can be added later once the basics are running.

5 Invoicing▶

Architecture jobs usually have two stage invoices on the quoted work. Extras quoted during the job are invoiced separately.

One option is a task for Josie whenever a stage needs invoicing. She ticks it off once the invoice is out, so everyone can see it has been done. This can be added after the first version if you prefer.

6 Your existing data▶
  • Live projects from your main project spreadsheet are imported in bulk, rather than typed in one at a time. The tracker for live architectural projects is used to fill in the detail.
  • Some live jobs sit under an old project number, such as a job that has come back after a few years. These need adding to the list.
  • When a job goes quiet, close the project. If the client comes back, reopen it, with everything still there.
  • The GDPR spreadsheet is not needed. The information will be on each record and searchable.
